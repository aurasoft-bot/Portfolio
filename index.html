How I work

Right first. Then fast.

Newsrooms taught me the order. A quick piece with one wrong fact does more harm than a slow piece that’s right, so I check before I file.

Plain beats clever.

If you have to reread a sentence, it isn’t finished. I’d rather be understood than admired.

Reader first, format second.

An article, a PR piece and a whitepaper do three different jobs for three different readers. I pick the reader before I pick the format.

Make the whole team sharper.

I like working with writers and editors. The best edit makes the piece better and leaves it sounding like the person who wrote it.

Build a voice that travels.

A brand voice is only useful if other people can write in it. I build it to still sound like you when I’m not at the keyboard.
